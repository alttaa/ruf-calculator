Start a RUF session:

1. Read `docs/PROGRESS.md` and the last 2 entries of `docs/SESSION_LOG.md`.
2. Run the test suite from `python/` (`python -m pytest -q`) and report the result in one line.
   If the venv isn't active or pytest is missing, tell Alex the exact commands to fix it.
3. Recap in 3–5 short bullets: what's done, what we were in the middle of, any open questions.
4. Ask Alex 2 quick warm-up questions about the last thing he learned, and wait for his answers.
5. Then propose the single next small step from PROGRESS.md and ask if he's ready.

Follow .claude/rules/teaching.md. Keep the whole recap short.
